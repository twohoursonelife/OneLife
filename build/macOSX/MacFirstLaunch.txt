FIRST LAUNCH ON MAC
====================

This build isn't signed by a paid Apple developer account, so macOS blocks
it the first time with a message like "cannot be opened because the
developer cannot be verified".  Clearing that takes two steps, and it's a
one-time thing.


STEP 1: Unzip, and move the folder out of Downloads
---------------------------------------------------

Double-click the downloaded .zip in Finder to unpack it.  That gives you a
folder named 2HOL_<version> containing the .app and the game's data folders
(objects, animations, sounds, and so on).

Now drag that whole folder somewhere outside Downloads -- your Applications
folder or your home folder both work fine.  Don't skip this: when an
unsigned app is launched straight out of Downloads, macOS runs it from a
hidden read-only copy of itself, where it can't see its own data folders,
and it will fail to start no matter what else you do.

Move the whole folder, not just the .app.  The game reads and writes its
data in whatever folder the .app is sitting in, so pulling the .app out by
itself breaks it.


STEP 2: Clear the quarantine flag
----------------------------------

Open Terminal (Applications -> Utilities -> Terminal), type this, with a
space at the end:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine 

then drag the 2HOL_<version> folder from Finder onto the Terminal window --
that fills in the correct path for you -- and press Return.  You'll see no
output; that means it worked.  Now double-click the .app.

This is the method to use.  macOS puts the quarantine flag on every file in
the download, including the libraries bundled inside the .app, and the
point-and-click approvals below only ever clear the .app itself.  That's
why the app can stay blocked, or quietly do nothing when opened, even after
you've approved it in System Settings.  The command above clears the whole
folder at once.


If you'd rather not use Terminal
---------------------------------

These sometimes work on their own, depending on your macOS version.  Try
them in order, and fall back to Step 2 if the app still won't start:

  - Right-click (or Control-click) the .app, choose "Open", then click
    "Open" again in the dialog.  (Removed in macOS 15 Sequoia and later.)

  - Try to open the app normally so it gets blocked, then go to System
    Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about the
    blocked app, and click "Open Anyway".


What you do NOT need to do
---------------------------

  - Don't grant the app Full Disk Access.  It never needs it, and it won't
    fix a Gatekeeper block.  If you already added it while trying to get
    the game running, go to System Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Full
    Disk Access and remove it.

  - Don't restart your Mac.  Nothing here takes effect at boot.

  - You shouldn't see a prompt asking to let the game access a folder.  If
    one does appear, allow it -- but its absence isn't the problem, and
    Step 2 is still what you're missing.


After this, the app opens normally from then on -- and keep that folder
intact, with the .app next to its data folders.  If you later move the game
somewhere else, move or copy the whole folder.
